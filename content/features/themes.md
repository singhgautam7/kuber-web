Make Kuber your own with curated accent palettes and full appearance customization:

- **Seven Curated Accent Families**: Choose from Signature (classic blue), Flewtube (vivid red), Woofsapp (fresh green), Purrhub (warm amber), Honkpe (deep purple), Squeakdin (slate navy), and Oinkzon (terracotta orange).
- **Appearance Modes**: Match your device dynamically with System mode, or lock in dedicated Light or Dark appearance.
- **Live Mini Swatch Previews**: Preview every theme directly within the picker sheet. Each card renders miniature transaction rows and home pills in that palette's exact color tokens.
- **Full-App Cohesive Recoloring**: Selecting a family instantly re-tints buttons, active navigation indicators, cards, and charts, while preserving clear contrast for expense and income indicators.
- **Free vs Pro Access**: The default Kuber Signature palette is completely free in both Light and Dark modes. Upgrading to Kuber Pro unlocks all additional accent families.
