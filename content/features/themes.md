Make Kuber your own with the same accent families as the app and full appearance customization:

- **Seventeen Accent Families**: Kuber Signature (classic blue), Mull, Vellum, Foxglove, Slate, Clay, Perch, Ember, Fern, Tide, Blush, Iris, Citron, Neon, Saffron, Sky and Ruby, each in light and dark.
- **Appearance Modes**: Follow your phone with System mode, or lock in Light or Dark. True black is available in dark mode for the families that support it.
- **From Your Wallpaper**: Let Kuber build a palette from your Android wallpaper colour.
- **Full-App Recoloring**: Picking a family instantly re-tints buttons, navigation, cards and charts, while income and expense stay clear.
- **Free and Pro**: Kuber Signature is free in light and dark. Kuber Pro unlocks the other sixteen families and the wallpaper palette.
