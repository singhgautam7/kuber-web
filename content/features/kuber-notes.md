Kuber Notes works just like a familiar notepad, but with powerful financial arithmetic built right into your text.

- **Inline Calculations**: Write expressions, discounts, or quantities like `220 * 4 =` and watch Kuber compute the result in real time.
- **Smart Totals**: Type `"total"` or `"="` on a new line and Kuber smartly calculates the sum of your listed items.
- **One-Tap Actions**: Tap any highlighted number or calculated total to instantly convert it into a transaction, a loan, or a lent/borrowed ledger entry.
- **Private & Protected**: Your notes live strictly on your device and can be secured with biometrics or app lock.
