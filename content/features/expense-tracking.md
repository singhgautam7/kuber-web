- Log income, expenses and transfers in seconds. As you type, Kuber suggests the category, account and merchant based on your previous transactions, so repeat entries are almost automatic. Recurring entries are supported too, and everything stays on your device.
- You can also use the calculator button for complex transactions, or attach pdf or images to the transactions.
