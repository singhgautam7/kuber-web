Plan your monthly spending and avoid overshooting category limits with clear progress indicators and timely alerts:

- **Category Spending Limits**: Create custom budget caps for specific expense categories such as Groceries, Dining Out, Entertainment, Shopping, or Utilities.
- **Live Progress Tracking**: Watch live progress bars fill up with every logged expense, showing total spent, remaining allowance, and days left in the billing period.
- **Custom Alert Thresholds**: Set up warnings at key milestones (like 50%, 80%, or 100% of your limit) or custom monetary amounts to receive notifications before you overspend.
- **Recurring Monthly Plans**: Budgets automatically reset and roll over at the start of each month, or can be configured for custom date spans.
- **Budget History and Cycles**: Review past monthly performance in the history sheet to see where spending stays disciplined and where limits need tuning.
- **Pause or Resume Anytime**: Temporarily pause a budget (for example during holidays or travel) without deleting its historical records.
- **Home Feed Widget**: Keep your active budgets front and center by enabling the Budgets widget directly on your dashboard.
