Speak naturally and Kuber transforms your words into transactions in seconds, completely hands-free.

- **Integrated into Quick Add**: Voice input is accessible directly through [Quick Add](/features/quick-add/). Tap the microphone icon on the home feed widget or switch to voice mode in full-screen Quick Add.
- **100% On-Device & Private**: Speech recognition runs entirely on your device. Audio never leaves your phone or touches external servers.
- **Natural Language Parsing**: Say everyday sentences like *"250 on groceries"* or *"400 for petrol"* and Kuber automatically fills in the amount, category, and date for your confirmation.
