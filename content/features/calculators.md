Plan, compare, and forecast your finances with a comprehensive suite of offline tools:

- **Save Any Calculation**: Bookmark results from any calculator to revisit later. Your saved scenarios (such as home loan estimates or investment goals) stay pinned right at the top for instant access.
- **Almost All Kinds of Calculators**: Choose from more than 25 dedicated calculators spanning every financial scenario:
  - **Borrowing**: EMI calculator, loan comparisons, and prepayment impact analysis.
  - **Tax and Salary**: In-depth salary breakdowns comparing old vs new tax regimes, HRA exemptions, and GST.
  - **Planning and Wealth**: Goal planners, retirement corpus estimates, and lumpsum vs SIP growth.
  - **Daily Utilities**: Bill splitters, currency converters, discount checks, and inflation math.
- **Visual Breakdowns and Schedules**: View principal vs interest donut charts, balance-over-time curves, and full yearly or monthly amortization schedules.
- **Completely Private and Offline**: All maths and saved records stay strictly on your phone with zero network calls.
