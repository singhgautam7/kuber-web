Plan, compare, and forecast your finances with a comprehensive suite of offline tools:

- **Save Any Calculation**: Bookmark results from any calculator to revisit later. Your saved scenarios (such as home loan estimates or investment goals) stay pinned right at the top for instant access.
- **18 Calculators in Four Groups**: The same groups as the Tools hub in the app:
  - **Finance Calculators**: EMI, investment returns, SIP amount, FD and RD, PPF, inflation, loan prepayment impact, and lumpsum vs SIP.
  - **Tax and Salary**: Salary breakdown with the old and new tax regimes, GST, and HRA exemption.
  - **Planning**: Goal planner and retirement corpus.
  - **Quick Calculators**: Bill splitter, currency converter, break-even, tip, and discount.
- **Visual Breakdowns and Schedules**: View principal vs interest donut charts, balance-over-time curves, and full yearly or monthly amortization schedules.
- **Private and Offline**: All maths and saved calculations stay on your phone. The Currency Converter is the one tool that goes online: it downloads exchange rates when you have a connection and keeps the last rates for offline use.
