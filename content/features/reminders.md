Never miss a bill, salary payout, or EMI deadline with custom financial reminders:

- **Track Anything Money-Related**: Schedule nudges for credit card bills, rent, maid salaries, loan EMIs, subscription renewals, or money you are owed.
- **Amounts, Types, and Categories**: Attach optional amounts, toggle between Expense and Income, assign categories, and add memory notes for full context.
- **Custom Times and Repeat Rules**: Set exact alert dates and times, with flexible repeat options (daily, weekly, monthly) for regular obligations.
- **Smart Filter Chips and Search**: Review what is coming up at a glance with one-tap filters for `All`, `Overdue`, `Today`, `This week`, and `Completed`, plus instant keyword search.
- **Private On-Device Notifications**: Alerts are scheduled locally on your phone through Android system notifications, keeping your payment schedules completely private.
