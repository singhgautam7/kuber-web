Keep your financial data protected and always up to date without lifting a finger:

- **Scheduled On-Device Copies**: Automatically create fresh backups in the background on your chosen schedule: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
- **Custom Destination Folder**: Choose exactly where backup files are saved on your phone (such as `Download / Kuber` or your chosen storage directory).
- **Smart Retention Limits**: Keep your storage tidy by retaining the last 1, 5, or 10 backups, with older versions pruned automatically.
- **Instant Manual Backups**: Run an immediate backup anytime with a single tap or let the automated scheduler run quietly in the background.
- **Zero Cloud, Total Privacy**: Your backups remain 100% on your device. Kuber has no cloud servers and no access to your exported files.
