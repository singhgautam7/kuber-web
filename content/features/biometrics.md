Lock Kuber and sensitive financial sections behind your device hardware authentication:

- **Fingerprint and Face Unlock**: Secure your finances with biometric sensors built into your smartphone for rapid, friction-free access.
- **App-Wide or Section Locks**: Require authentication when opening the app, or gate specific sensitive tools such as Kuber Notes and Kuber Cards.
- **Hardware-Level Keystore**: Powered directly by Android BiometricPrompt APIs and secure hardware enclaves.
- **Zero Exposure**: Biometric templates never enter the application. Authentication is handled entirely by your operating system, keeping your identity private.
