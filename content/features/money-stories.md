Experience your financial updates through bite-sized, Instagram and WhatsApp-style stories:

- **Bite-Sized Visual Stories**: Tap through glanceable, full-screen story cards with progress bars and bold visual highlights that make reviewing your money effortless.
- **Covers Everything You Track**: Automatically generates recaps for daily summaries, weekly reviews, monthly category breakdowns, and smart behavioural patterns (such as weekend spending habits).
- **48-Hour Home Feed Presence**: Fresh stories appear right at the top of your Home tab and remain accessible for 48 hours so you never miss an update.
- **Permanent Stories Archive**: Once a story expires from your home feed, it is safely stored in the Stories Archive. Revisit the complete history of all your past recaps whenever you want.
- **Easy Sharing and Total Privacy**: Share recap cards directly with friends or family, with all story computations happening strictly offline on your device.
