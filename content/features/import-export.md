Take complete ownership of your personal finance data with versatile local import, export, and sandbox capabilities:

- **Full Data Ownership**: Your records belong exclusively to you, stored securely on your phone with zero vendor lock-in.
- **Export Transaction Statements (History Tab)**:
  - Generate filtered transaction statements directly from the History tab.
  - Export in spreadsheet-ready **CSV** (for analysis in Excel, Google Sheets, or custom scripts) or beautifully formatted **PDF** (with monthly summaries, category totals, and debit/credit styling).
- **Full Database Backups (Settings -> Data Management)**:
  - **Export Data**: Create complete snapshots of your accounts, categories, tags, transactions, loans, investments, and recurring rules in structured JSON or CSV format.
  - **Import Data**: Seamlessly restore previously exported backups or migrate from external expense tools with one tap.
- **Sandbox Mock Data**:
  - **Generate Mock Data**: Populate Kuber with realistic mock accounts, categories, and test transactions to explore charts, heatmaps, and financial health scores without manually typing data.
  - **Clear Mock Data**: Wipe test records cleanly at any time from the Danger Zone to begin logging your genuine finances.
- **100% Private and Offline**: All statement generation, JSON serialization, and CSV parsing run locally on your phone without ever uploading files to external cloud servers.
