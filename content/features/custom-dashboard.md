Custom Dashboard turns your transactions into a dashboard you design yourself. Pick exactly which transactions to look at, see the totals, split them into groups and add the charts that answer your question. Then save it and open it again with one tap.

### Filters You Can Type or Pick
- **Type it, or tap it**: Type `category:food amount>500` straight into the filter box, or pick Type, Amount, Category, Account, Tag, Date, Has, Source or Words from the list and choose the values. Both add to the same box, where your cursor is.
- **And, or, not and brackets**: Tap the word "and" between two filters to make it "or". Use the `( ) and or not` bar to build filters like `type:expense and (category:Dining or category:Fuel)`. Brackets show as a shaded box, so you can always see what goes together.
- **Comparisons**: `amount>500`, `amount<500`, `amount>=500`, `amount=500` and `amount:500..2000` all work, and so do dates like `date:2026-03` or `date>2026-03`. Both `:` and `=` work.
- **Suggestions while you type**: Field names, your categories, accounts and tags appear as you type, with how many transactions each one has.
- **In plain words**: Kuber reads your filter back as a sentence, so you know exactly what it matches.
- **How to use**: A built-in guide explains every keyword with an example.

### Totals, Groups and Sorting
- **KPI cards**: Transactions, income, expense, net, average, attachments and the largest transaction for the current filter. Choose which cards to show and in what order.
- **Group by**: Category, account, tag, type, source, month or year. Each group shows its count, total and share, and opens its own list of transactions.
- **Sort**: Newest or oldest, highest or lowest, or A to Z.
- **Stays out of the way**: The Filter, Group, Sort and Saved buttons fold into one slim bar as you scroll.

### Up to 4 Charts
- **Donut**: Share by category, account, tag, type or source. Tap a slice to see its share, the exact amount and View transactions.
- **Bars and line**: Totals, counts or averages, split any way you like. Tap a bar or point for a tooltip with View transactions. Long charts scroll sideways, like the Home chart.
- **Stacked bars and multiple lines**: Compare up to 3 categories, accounts, tags or types month by month, stacked in one bar or drawn as one line each.
- **Calendar heatmap**: One square per day for the last year of your results, darker on the days you spent more. Tap a day to see its total and its transactions.
- **Move, edit and remove**: Drag charts into the order you want. Every chart follows your filter as it changes.

### Saved Views
- **Save the whole dashboard**: Filter, grouping, sorting, charts and KPI cards are saved together under a name.
- **Open it again in one tap**: Your saved views are listed on the start page and in the Saved button.
- **Know what changed**: Change a saved view and Kuber tells you what is different, with Save changes or Save as new.
- **Start fresh any time**: Leave the app and the next visit starts on a clean page, or tap Start fresh in Saved views.
- **In your backup**: Saved views are included in your JSON backup.

### Export
Export exactly the transactions you are looking at as a CSV file, in the same columns as the History export.

### Private by Design
Every filter, chart and saved view is worked out on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is needed.
