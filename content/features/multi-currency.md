Manage your finances in the currency that fits your life, with simple global configuration and dedicated conversion tools:

- **Single Primary Currency Tracking**: Kuber currently tracks all accounts, transactions, budgets, and investments in one chosen primary currency at a time. This keeps your net worth calculations, analytics, and category rollups completely consistent and accurate.
- **Global Currency Selection**: Choose from any worldwide currency in Settings, including INR (₹), USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), JPY (¥), CAD, AUD, AED, SGD, and dozens more.
- **Localized Symbols and Separators**: Switching your primary currency automatically updates currency symbols, comma separators, and decimal formatting across every dashboard, card, and report.
- **Built-In Currency Converter**: When traveling abroad or checking foreign prices, use the built-in Currency Converter under Tools & Calculators for instant conversions between world currencies.
- **Always Free and Offline**: Currency selection is completely free for all users and works entirely on-device without requiring account logins or cloud syncing.
