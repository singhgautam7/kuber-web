Automate your regular cash flow and keep your records completely up to date without daily manual logging:

- **Automated Inflows and Outflows**: Set up recurring rules for regular credits (monthly salary, freelancing retainers, rental income) or regular debits (Netflix subscriptions, gym memberships, house rent, utility bills).
- **Flexible Recurrence Frequencies**: Choose schedules that match your obligations:
  - **Standard Intervals**: Daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or yearly.
  - **Custom Day Cycles**: Specify exact custom intervals (such as every 45 days).
- **Monthly Net Cash Flow at a Glance**: The dedicated overview card shows your net monthly recurring balance (total monthly income minus recurring expenses) and alerts you to upcoming charges due in the next few days.
- **Background Auto-Execution**: Kuber automatically logs the transaction when the due date arrives, crediting or debiting the selected bank account or card with the correct category.
- **End Conditions and Lifespans**: Keep rules active indefinitely, or automatically stop them after a set number of occurrences or on a specific end date.
- **Pause and Resume Any Time**: Temporarily halt rules (like a paused gym membership) without deleting them, preserving your setup until you are ready to resume.
- **Connected to Upcoming Events**: Recurring rules feed seamlessly into the Upcoming Events calendar and widget, so you always know when your next debit or salary credit is approaching.
