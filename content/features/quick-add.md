Log expenses in seconds by typing or speaking in plain language:

- **Home Feed Widget**: Enter transactions directly from the home tab widget or jump into full-screen mode anytime.
- **Multi-Line Parsing**: Type multiple entries at once (e.g. `300 in movies` and `650 in trip`). Kuber detects each amount and assigns or suggests new categories automatically.
- **On-Device Voice Add**: Tap to speak naturally. Speech processing runs locally on your device with complete privacy.
