Plan ahead and stay on top of every upcoming obligation with a single unified timeline:

- **Aggregated Across the Entire App**: Kuber automatically gathers scheduled events from all your modules so you never have to check multiple screens:
  - **Credit Cards**: Bill statement generation dates and payment due dates.
  - **Investments**: Upcoming SIP schedules and periodic deposits.
  - **Loans**: Monthly EMI installments and due dates.
  - **Lent and Borrowed**: Repayment deadlines for personal debts and shared IOUs.
  - **Recurring Transactions**: Subscriptions, gym memberships, rent, and expected salary inflows.
  - **Custom Reminders**: One-off alerts for utility bills, maid salaries, and dues.
- **Smart Time Horizons**: Toggle your forecast window across the **Next 7 days**, **Next 30 days**, or **Next 90 days** to manage short-term cash flow and plan for upcoming quarters.
- **Source Filter Chips**: Filter the timeline by source type with quick chips for Reminders, EMIs, SIPs, Recurring, Credit Cards, and Lent / Borrowed.
- **Chronological Breakdown**: Events are grouped clearly by timeframe (Next Week, Later) with color-coded type badges, exact amounts, and dates.
- **100% Offline and Private**: Aggregated on the fly from your local device database without sharing your financial schedule with external services.
