Conceal your financial figures in an instant with Kuber Privacy Mode:

- **One-Tap Instant Masking**: Tap the eye icon in the top header on your Home tab to replace balances, income, expenses, and daily averages with asterisks (`****`).
- **Shoulder-Surfer Protection**: Safely open the app in crowded public spaces, coffee shops, or public transit without revealing sensitive account balances.
- **Persistent Local State**: Your privacy preference stays active as you navigate between screens until you choose to reveal numbers again.
- **Zero Cloud, Total Privacy**: Masking is handled purely in the local UI presentation layer without altering your underlying financial records.
