Manage your personal finances comfortably in your native tongue with extensive regional language support:

- **9 Languages (Beta)**: English and 8 Indian languages:
  - **English**
  - **हिन्दी (Hindi)**
  - **मराठी (Marathi)**
  - **ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (Punjabi)**
  - **বাংলা (Bengali)**
  - **తెలుగు (Telugu)**
  - **தமிழ் (Tamil)**
  - **മലയാളം (Malayalam)**
  - **ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada)**
- **Native Script Rendering**: All buttons, categories, transaction types, analytical summaries, and system dialogs render cleanly in native regional typography.
- **One-Tap Switching**: Change languages instantly inside Settings without needing to restart the app or migrate any data.
- **Active Beta Translations**: Regional localizations are actively evolving in beta, continuously refined based on user suggestions and real-world usage.
- **Private and Completely Offline**: Every translation dictionary is bundled directly with the application, ensuring your localized experience requires zero network requests.
