Keep full control over your borrowings, EMI timelines, and outstanding balances in one organized dashboard:

- **Support for All Loan Types**: Manage any kind of loan structure with dedicated categorization:
  - **Home Loans**: Long-term housing and mortgage loans.
  - **Vehicle Loans**: Car, bike, and EV financing.
  - **Personal Loans**: Unsecured bank loans and personal lines of credit.
  - **Education Loans**: Tuition, college, and study financing.
  - **Other Custom Loans**: Business financing, equipment loans, or private agreements.
- **Automated EMI Deductions**: Enable automatic EMI logging on your designated bill date (days 1 to 28), debiting the linked bank account and tagging payments under the Loan EMI category.
- **Comprehensive Loan Details**:
  - **Lender Information**: Keep the bank or institution name clearly visible.
  - **Reference and Account Numbers**: Save your loan account number or sanction reference for quick lookup.
  - **Interest Rates and Types**: Track fixed or floating interest rates per annum alongside principal amounts.
  - **Repayment Horizons**: Monitor start dates, bill dates, and projected completion dates.
- **Visual Debt Dashboard**: Monitor total outstanding debt, total amount paid, percentage cleared, and next scheduled EMI dates across all active loans.
- **Reminders and Timeline Integration**: Upcoming EMI due dates feed directly into your device notifications and the Upcoming Events timeline so you never miss an installment.
- **Notes and Loan Closure**: Record notes for prepayment terms or sanction details, and mark loans as completed once the balance is cleared.
