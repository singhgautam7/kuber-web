About Kuber Pro
close
Kuber Pro unlocks every feature and helps fund development. No account is needed and nothing moves to the cloud.
workspace_premium
What Kuber Pro is
One subscription or a one-time purchase unlocks every gated feature. Everything stays on your device, offline, with no account.
shop
Billing is Google Play
Google Play handles payment. Kuber never sees your card or payment details. Prices are shown by Play in your local currency.
hourglass_top
Free trial
New yearly customers get a 14-day free trial through Google Play. You are charged only when the trial ends, and you can cancel before then.
cancel
Cancel anytime
Cancel from the Play Store subscriptions screen. You keep Pro until the end of the current period. Nothing is deleted when Pro ends.
devices
Restore across devices
Restore purchases works on any device signed in to the same Google account. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
local_cafe
Supporting Kuber
The Buy me a coffee tips are a one-time thank you. They unlock no features and are not a subscription.
