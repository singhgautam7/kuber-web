Home v3.2 · How Kuber works

An optional 5-page animated tour, built on Home v3.1; where they differ, Home v3.2 wins. It teaches one habit (note every expense) and then shows what Kuber does with it. Reached from the last Welcome page, a one-time Home card, and More. Shown in Signature, light and dark; every colour is a theme role, so the other 6 palettes follow. Specs next to this file: entry-points.md, storyboards.md, changelog.md, copy.md, open-decisions.md.

HV32.1

Home v3.2 · Entry points

Three ways in. The tour is never part of the required path. Rules for when each appears are in entry-points.md.

a · Welcome, last page
A text button under the chips, far from Continue and lower in emphasis. Chip choices are saved before the tour opens. Skip or back returns to this page; Start using Kuber finishes onboarding and opens Home.
