Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 March 2026

Kuber respects your privacy. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app. The short version: your data stays on your device.

No cloud storage. No data collection. No tracking. Full control stays with you.

Information we collect

Kuber is a local-first application. We do not collect or store personal information (name, email, phone), financial data on external servers, location data, or your contacts and media.

Local data storage

All your data, such as transactions, accounts, categories and analytics, is stored locally using a secure on-device database (Isar). We do not transmit or store your data on any remote servers.

Biometrics

Kuber may use fingerprint or face unlock to secure access. Biometric data is handled entirely by your device's operating system. Kuber does not access, store or share it.

SMS access

Kuber may request permission to read SMS to detect bank and payment transaction alerts and suggest them for import.

WHAT WE READ
Only messages from recognised bank sender IDs (HDFCBK, SBIINB, ICICIB, AXISBK). Transaction content only.
WHAT WE NEVER DO
Transmit SMS off device, store raw SMS, share with third parties, or read SMS in the background.

Detected details are parsed locally and shown for your review. You choose what to import. Revoke access anytime in Android Settings; this disables only SMS import.

Data deletion

Because everything is on-device, you delete your data by clearing app data in device settings, or by uninstalling the app. There is nothing to request from us.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the developer, Gautam Rajeev Singh, via the in-app feedback screen or the developer site.
