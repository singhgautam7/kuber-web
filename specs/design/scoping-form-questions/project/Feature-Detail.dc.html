SMS Import

Pro

Turn bank and payment SMS into transactions. Parsed on your device, imported only when you approve.

SCREENSHOTS Shown in your selected theme where available

How it works

When you open the SMS Import screen, Kuber scans messages from recognised Indian bank and payment sender IDs, such as HDFCBK, SBIINB and ICICIB. It reads only transaction alerts, never personal messages or OTPs.

Each detected message is parsed into an amount, type, date and merchant name where available. You review the list and pick what to import. Nothing is added without your explicit approval.

Good to know
SMS is read on-device and on-demand only. Kuber never reads SMS in the background and never sends message content off your phone.

What Kuber does not do

  • Transmit any SMS content off your device
  • Store raw SMS anywhere in the app
  • Share content with third parties or analytics
Permission note
You can revoke SMS access anytime in Android Settings. This disables import but nothing else in Kuber.
