BOILERPLATE DESCRIPTION

Kuber is a fully offline personal finance app for India. It tracks income, expenses and transfers, imports bank SMS on-device, and reads your spending back with analytics and insights. There is no account, no cloud and no tracking. Your data never leaves your phone. Kuber is under 17 MB, supports 5 languages and ships with 7 themes across light and dark.

Short version: Kuber is a fast, private, offline expense tracker made in India.
